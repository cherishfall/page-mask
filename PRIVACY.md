# PageMask Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-26_

PageMask ("the Extension") respects your privacy completely. This policy explains, in plain language, what the Extension does and does not do with data.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The Extension contains no analytics, no trackers, no advertising, and makes no network requests of any kind. There is no server, no database, and no account system.

## What is stored, and where

Your configuration — which sites you enabled, which elements you chose to hide, your preferred tab titles/icons — is stored **only in your browser's local extension storage** (`chrome.storage.local`). It never leaves your device.

## Permissions

- `storage` — saves your configuration locally.
- `scripting` and `activeTab` — apply the hiding styles you created to the tabs you use them on.
- Optional host permissions — requested **one site at a time**, only when you explicitly enable the Extension for that site. You can revoke any site at any time from the Extension's management page.

The Extension runs only on sites you have explicitly added. It injects nothing anywhere else.

## Removal

Uninstalling the Extension (or using its per-site "reset" feature) deletes all related data from your browser.
